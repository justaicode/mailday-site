Mailday

Your email and calendar, calm and fast.

Mailday is a native email app for Mac, iPhone and iPad. It brings your Gmail and IMAP accounts together in one inbox, with Smart Mailboxes, snooze, scheduled sending, reminders and your calendar beside your mail.

Your mail is downloaded straight from your email provider to your devices. Mailday does not show ads and does not sell or share your data.
